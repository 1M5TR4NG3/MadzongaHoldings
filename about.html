About Us

Registered, compliant, and ready to build.

Madzonga Holdings is a CIPC-registered construction group based in Kempton Park, Gauteng, built around one idea: a client should get one accountable team for building, civil and installation work instead of juggling three contractors.

Our story

Madzonga Holdings (Pty) Ltd was registered with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission in April 2024 and is based at 15 Johannes Street, Kempton Park West, Gauteng. The company was founded by Chrispen Madzonga to bring building, civil, renovation and electrical work under one accountable team, rather than clients having to run separate contractors for each part of a project.

The group is also registered with SARS as a company and provisional taxpayer, and works to the standards set out in the Companies Act and the Occupational Health and Safety Act on every site.

The approach is simple: one accountable site manager, a fixed-scope quote before work starts, and a team that shows up when it says it will.
