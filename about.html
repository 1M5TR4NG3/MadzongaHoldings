Our story

Founded in 2010 in Johannesburg, Madzonga Holdings began as a small building contractor working on residential renovations across the East Rand. As repeat clients started asking for electrical and civil work on the same sites, the founders brought those skills in-house rather than subcontracting them out.

Today the group operates across Gauteng, with project experience extending into Limpopo and the Western Cape, employing over 60 permanent staff and a rotating base of trade subcontractors sized to each job.

From residential renovations in Sandton to a 48-unit housing scheme in Soweto, the common thread is the same: one accountable site manager, a fixed-scope quote, and a team that shows up when it says it will.
